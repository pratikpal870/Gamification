Gamification Strategies for Enhancing Collaboration in Virtual Teams and Improving Performance in Esports

This PhD proposal investigates how gamification can reduce collaboration challenges in geographically distributed esports teams, and whether collaboration gains translate into measurable performance outcomes [one].

Abstract Summary

Esports success depends heavily on teamwork, communication, and trust. Virtual teams face barriers that weaken collaboration and coordination. This proposal examines gamification as an intervention to strengthen team interaction and engagement, and then evaluates its relationship to performance outcomes [one] [five].

Objectives Goals

  • Analyze gamification frameworks and identify elements suitable for esports team collaboration [one] [seven].
  • Assess collaboration dynamics (communication, trust, coordination) in virtual esports teams [six].
  • Develop tailored gamification strategies aligned to team needs and motivations [three] [eight].
  • Evaluate impact on collaboration and link to measurable performance indicators [nine].

Methodology Headlines open as buttons

Phase One — Literature Review
  • Gamified learning and theory building [one].
  • Motivation and engagement styles in game contexts [three].
  • Collaboration in VR / esports-oriented learning settings [six].
Phase Two — Design of Gamification Strategies
  • Design gamification elements for cooperative and competitive teamwork behavior [eight].
  • Apply approaches for innovation teams and collaboration incentives [seven].
  • Consider IT service management gamification lessons as structured guidance [four].
Phase Three — Data Collection & Evaluation
  • Use valid measurement instruments and reliability checks where relevant [two].
  • Evaluate performance outcomes and collaboration impacts in gamified settings [nine].
Phase Four — Analysis & Reporting
  • Link collaboration metrics to performance outcomes and interpret results using theory and evidence [one] [nine].

Timeline Four-year plan

Year One — Review & Research Design
  • Deep literature review, framework selection, ethics preparation, and measurement planning.
Year Two — Prototype & Pilot Studies
  • Design gamification strategies, run pilot studies, refine instrumentation and interventions.
Year Three — Full Study & Data Collection
  • Deploy interventions, collect quantitative and qualitative data, iterate where needed.
Year Four — Final Analysis & Dissertation
  • Finalize analysis, write dissertation, publish papers, present at conferences.
